# =================================================================================================
# EN: THE PEACE MENU OF THE GUERRA GRANDE
#
#     The war ends in one of several ways, and the two governments do not end it the same way,
#     because they did not want the same thing. Everything offered below is something one of the
#     two actually proposed, decreed, signed or asked a foreign power for, between 1838 and 1852.
#     The sources are in ~/dev/juegos/victoria3-uruguay/SOURCES_peace.md, with URLs, and nothing
#     in this file goes past them. Where the record is disputed -- most of all on whether Oribe
#     ever meant to hand the Republic to Rosas -- the text says so instead of picking a side.
#
#     The menus are deliberately NOT symmetrical. The Defensa won by surviving until someone else
#     broke Rosas, and its documented programme is foreign: Brazilian treaties, an Anglo-French
#     protectorate, and a campaign across the river. The Cerrito would have won by walking into
#     the city, and its documented programme is domestic and legalist: the interrupted
#     presidency, the Constitution of 1830, the acts of its own government declared lawful --
#     with reincorporation into the Confederation sitting there as an offer it never answered.
#
# ES: EL MENU DE PAZ DE LA GUERRA GRANDE
#
#     La guerra termina de varias maneras, y los dos gobiernos no la terminan igual, porque no
#     querian lo mismo. Todo lo que se ofrece abajo es algo que uno de los dos propuso, decreto,
#     firmo o le pidio a una potencia extranjera entre 1838 y 1852. Las fuentes estan en
#     ~/dev/juegos/victoria3-uruguay/SOURCES_peace.md, con URLs, y nada en este archivo va mas
#     alla de ellas. Donde el registro esta en disputa -- sobre todo si Oribe penso alguna vez en
#     entregarle la Republica a Rosas -- el texto lo dice en vez de elegir bando.
#
#     Los menus NO son simetricos a proposito. La Defensa gano sobreviviendo hasta que otro
#     rompio a Rosas, y su programa documentado es de afuera: tratados con el Brasil, un
#     protectorado anglofrances y una campania del otro lado del rio. El Cerrito habria ganado
#     entrando a la ciudad, y su programa documentado es de adentro y legalista: la presidencia
#     interrumpida, la Constitucion de 1830, los actos de su propio gobierno declarados legales
#     -- con la reincorporacion a la Confederacion ahi, como una oferta que nunca contesto.
# =================================================================================================


# -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# EN: gg_peace.1 -- THE DEFENSA DICTATES THE PEACE.
#     Reached when the government marked as the Defensa has the war won: it replaces the single
#     fork of guerra_grande.8. The historical outcome is option a; everything else is a road the
#     men of Montevideo actually proposed and did not get, or got later.
# ES: gg_peace.1 -- LA DEFENSA DICTA LA PAZ.
#     Se llega cuando el gobierno marcado como la Defensa tiene la guerra ganada: reemplaza la
#     bifurcacion unica de guerra_grande.8. Lo historico es la opcion a; lo demas son caminos que
#     los hombres de Montevideo propusieron de verdad y no consiguieron, o consiguieron despues.
# -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

		NOT = { has_global_variable = gg_peace_settled }

		#     which left the event unable to ever fire after a decisive military conquest (the
		#     real war annexing the loser outright, before our own score reached the peace
		#     threshold). Dropped: the immediate's random_country below already handles "the
		#     loser is already gone" gracefully by leaving scope:gg_peace_loser unset, and every
		#     option that annexes it is now guarded to skip cleanly when that happens.
		# ES: HOTFIX 2026-09-16 -- esto exigia que el pais del perdedor siguiera existiendo, lo
		#     que dejaba al evento sin poder disparar nunca despues de una conquista militar
		#     decisiva (la guerra real anexando al perdedor de una, antes de que nuestro propio
		#     puntaje llegara al piso de paz). Sacado: el random_country del immediate de abajo
		#     ya maneja bien que el perdedor ya no este, dejando el scope sin poner, y cada
		#     opcion que lo anexa ahora se salta limpio cuando eso pasa.
		has_variable = guerra_grande_defensa_government
	}

	immediate = {

							has_template = URU_Manuel_Oribe

			limit = { has_global_variable = gg_reunification_pledged }
			add_modifier = { name = gg_peace_oribe_sold_country years = 10 is_decaying = yes }
		}

	# EN: a -- THE FORMULA. 8 October 1851, the camp on the Pantanoso. The clause is the one that
	#     was signed: "Se declara que entre las diferentes opiniones en que han estado divididos
	#     los orientales, no habra vencidos ni vencedores." It came with a price the phrase hides:
	#     equal rights and equal claim to public office for every oriental whatever side he had
	#     been on, and the Republic assuming the war debt the Cerrito had run up. Oribe was not
	#     exiled -- a delegation of citizens asked Urquiza for guarantees for him and Urquiza gave
	#     them, and he stayed in his quinta, unmolested, until he died in 1857.
	# ES: a -- LA FORMULA. 8 de octubre de 1851, el cuartel del Pantanoso. La clausula es la que se
	#     firmo. Vino con un precio que la frase esconde: iguales derechos y opcion a los empleos

	#     le pidio garantias a Urquiza, Urquiza las dio, y se quedo en su quinta hasta 1857.

			name = guerra_grande_no_victors_no_vanquished
			years = 10

		}

			name = gg_peace_national_cockade

		}

	# EN: b -- THE DECREES OF FEBRUARY 1843, CARRIED OUT. This is not invented and it is not the
	#     mirror image of anything: it is the Defensa's own law, applied at last to the country it
	#     had never been able to reach. 10 February 1843: anyone in contact with the besiegers to a
	#     military commission, anyone materially helping them shot. 12 February: every oriental
	#     taken with arms in hand or wearing the enemy's divisa shot "en el acto y por la espalda".
	#     1 March: the families of the men in the invading army expelled from the city, some of
	#     them to the isla Libertad. The confiscations were the Defensa's first -- the Cerrito's
	#     decree of June 1845 says so in its own preamble, answering "una decision similar adoptada
	#     por el Gobierno de la Defensa". And there is a precedent for doing it rather than saying
	#     it: after Caseros, the new government in Buenos Aires handed the authors of the degollinas
	#     of 1840 and 1842 to the courts. Eduardo Acevedo marks that as "una diferencia notable" with
	#     what Urquiza imposed on this side of the river. This option is that difference, taken here.
	# ES: b -- LOS DECRETOS DE FEBRERO DE 1843, CUMPLIDOS. Esto no esta inventado y no es el espejo
	#     de nada: es la ley propia de la Defensa, aplicada por fin al pais que nunca habia podido
	#     alcanzar. 10 de febrero de 1843: a comision militar el que tenga trato con los sitiadores,
	#     fusilado el que los auxilie materialmente. 12 de febrero: fusilado "en el acto y por la
	#     espalda" todo oriental tomado con armas o con la divisa enemiga. 1 de marzo: expulsadas de
	#     la ciudad las familias de los del ejercito invasor, algunas a la isla Libertad. Las
	#     confiscaciones las empezo la Defensa: el decreto del Cerrito de junio de 1845 lo dice en su
	#     propio preambulo, contestando "una decision similar adoptada por el Gobierno de la
	#     Defensa". Y hay precedente de hacerlo y no solo decirlo: despues de Caseros el nuevo
	#     gobierno de Buenos Aires entrego a los tribunales a los autores de las degollinas de 1840 y
	#     1842, y Eduardo Acevedo lo marca como "una diferencia notable" con lo que impuso Urquiza de
	#     este lado. Esta opcion es esa diferencia, tomada aca.

		# EN/ES: T8, FE-12/FE-13 -- a pledge restricts the menu / el compromiso recorta el menu

		# EN/ES: T1, C6 -- the closing mark goes before any annex (I5).
		set_global_variable = gg_peace_settled

			name = gg_peace_blancos_proscribed
			years = 15
			is_decaying = yes
		}

		# The confiscations stand, and they are worth money.
		add_treasury = 250000
		change_infamy = 15

		# The countryside, which spent nine years under Oribe, pays for it.

			limit = {
				NOT = { state_region = s:STATE_MONTEVIDEO }

			value = large_radicals

			value = medium_radicals

			base = 10

	# EN: c -- THE FIVE TREATIES. Andres Lamas signed them in Rio de Janeiro on 12 October 1851,
	#     four days after the formula. Alliance, with Brazil guarantor of the Constitution and a
	#     right to intervene to restore order; subsidies against the customs revenue; limits, on
	#     the Cuareim, renouncing the San Ildefonso claim and leaving a dry coast on the Laguna
	#     Merin and the Yaguaron for fifty-eight years; commerce, ten years' exemption for
	#     Brazilian tasajo and for cattle driven north; extradition of fugitive slaves, against the
	#     Republic's own abolition. Contemporaries called it a diplomatic triumph and the country
	#     spent the next fourteen years arguing about it; in December 1864 a Uruguayan government
	#     burned the treaties in a public square, and in February 1865 the next one restored them.
	# ES: c -- LOS CINCO TRATADOS. Andres Lamas los firmo en Rio el 12 de octubre de 1851, cuatro
	#     dias despues de la formula. Alianza, con el Brasil garante de la Constitucion y derecho a
	#     intervenir; socorros contra la renta de aduana; limites en el Cuareim, renuncia a San
	#     Ildefonso y costa seca sobre la Merin y el Yaguaron por cincuenta y ocho anios; comercio,
	#     diez anios de exoneracion al tasajo brasilero y al ganado que cruce; extradicion de
	#     esclavos fugados, contra la propia abolicion. Los contemporaneos lo llamaron un triunfo
	#     diplomatico y el pais se paso catorce anios discutiendolo: en diciembre de 1864 un

	# EN: d -- THE PROTECTORATE ASKED FOR. Manuel Herrera y Obes, foreign minister of the Defensa,
	#     put a joint Anglo-French protectorate to the two powers in 1848. It was the fourth such
	#     request in ten years: Francisco Jose Munioz asked London in 1838 for an arrangement like
	#     the one over the Ionian Islands; chancellor Vidal tried again through Jose Ellauri in
	#     1841; Florencio Varela was sent to London in 1843-44. Separately, men of the French colony
	#     in Montevideo pushed a French protectorate with the goodwill of leading men of the Defensa
	#     and the backing of the French ambassador in Rio. Every one of these was refused -- which
	#     is why this option does not resolve here. It resolves in gg_peace.3.

	#     diez anios: Francisco Jose Munioz le pidio a Londres en 1838 un arreglo como el de las
