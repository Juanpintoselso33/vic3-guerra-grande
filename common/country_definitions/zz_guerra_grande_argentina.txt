# EN: THE PROVINCES AS COUNTRIES (22/9/2026, decided with Juan). None of them exists at game start and
#     none is ever born by pronouncing: a province becomes a country only when Rosas BEATS the country
#     that holds it. The beaten country splits into its provinces, each a protectorate of Buenos Aires
#     under its governor (gg_province_split_off in common/scripted_effects/gg_provinces.txt); what is
#     left of the Coalition changes tag to the province of its capital (Tucuman). Santa Fe, Santiago
#     del Estero, Mendoza, San Juan and San Luis never withdraw the mandate: their definitions exist
#     only for the day one of them is beaten inside another country.
#     TAGS VERIFIED FREE on 22/9/2026 against vanilla 1.13, every downloaded Workshop item and every
#     local mod (country_definitions, history, events, localization): TUC SLT JUJ CTM LRJ CBA SFE SDE
#     MZA SJN SNL. SLU is NOT free (Workshop 2898469548 defines it), so San Luis is SNL.
#     Colours are map colours only, chosen to tell neighbours apart. Flags (decided with Juan 22/9):
#     real Wikimedia flags by date and side, in common/flag_definitions/zz_guerra_grande_flags.txt.

#     protectorado de Buenos Aires con su gobernador; lo que queda de la Coalicion cambia de tag a la
