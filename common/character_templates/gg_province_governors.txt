#     the provinces Buenos Aires beats (gg_pact_governors.txt). Created only when their term begins, once
#     (gg_pact_gov_pick_or_create), in whoever holds the province.
#     WHAT IS SOURCED AND WHAT IS NOT. Names, provinces and dates come from
#     docs/guerra-grande-argentina/gobernadores-provincias.md. A birth date is written only when the
#     source gives one; with only the year it is 1 January of that year (said on the line), and with
#     «ca.» or nothing it is left out. No traits: the source documents none, and trait_generation is
#     empty so the engine does not invent them. No portraits yet (dna): out of scope on 22/9/2026.
#     THE INTEREST GROUP AND IDEOLOGY ARE THE MOD'S READING of the «bando» column, one rule for all:
#     rosista -> landowners, authoritarian; federal, antirrosista federal or unknown -> landowners,
#     moderate; Coalition or unitarian -> intelligentsia, radical (as Avellaneda already had).
#     Politicians only, never generals: none of them is given a command the source does not show.

#     vez, en quien tenga la provincia. Nombres, provincias y fechas: gobernadores-provincias.md. La
#     fecha de nacimiento va solo si la fuente la da; con el anio solo, es el 1 de enero (dicho en la
#     linea); con «ca.» o nada, no va. Sin traits ni retrato. El grupo de interes y la ideologia son la
#     lectura del mod de la columna «bando», con una sola regla para todos.
