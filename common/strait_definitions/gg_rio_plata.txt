#     Verified in game 21/9/2026: a modded strait counts trade once a bank has control, and closing
#     needs a naval fortification. The three full-control flags are "no" so one bank alone can close
#     it: with "yes", Rosas could never close while Montevideo was the Defensa's.
#     Magellan has no forts in 1836; its banks get control by modifier (gg_rio_plata on_actions).
#     The adjacency lines are in map_data/adjacencies.csv (full vanilla copy plus these two).
